The hosted service runs at [app.opendesk.sh](https://app.opendesk.sh). It is the same code as the self-hosted install, operated for you, so there is nothing to install. Plan for about ten minutes.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create your account">

Open [app.opendesk.sh/register](https://app.opendesk.sh/register).

<Screenshot src="/guides/start/sign-up" alt="The Create your account page: the sign-up buttons ringed as 1, the name, work email and password fields ringed as 2, and the Create account button ringed as 3" />

<Legend>

1. **Sign up with a provider**, such as Google or Microsoft, and skip the password.
2. Or fill in your **name**, **work email** and a **password** of 12 to 128 characters. Known breached passwords are refused; the bar under the field shows how strong yours is.
3. **Create account**.

</Legend>

</Step>
<Step title="Name your workspace">

A workspace holds your team's tickets, contacts, mailboxes, help center and settings. You become its owner, and you can rename it later. Your help center gets an address from the name; change it or [use your own domain](/guides/help-center-addresses/) whenever you like, from **Knowledge base, Help center settings**.

<Screenshot src="/guides/start/workspace" alt="Create your workspace: the workspace name field ringed as 1 and Continue ringed as 2" />

<Legend>

1. **Workspace name**: your company or your support team, such as Margin.
2. **Continue**.

</Legend>

</Step>
<Step title="Connect a mailbox">

Type the address your customers write to. opendesk looks up who hosts it and offers the right way to connect. You forward the address's mail to opendesk, where it becomes tickets, and your replies go out from the same address.

<Screenshot src="/guides/start/mailbox" alt="Connect your first mailbox: the name and email address fields ringed as 1, the detected provider and the ways to connect ringed as 2, Continue ringed as 3 and Skip for now ringed as 4" />

<Legend>

1. **Name** the mailbox for your team, such as Care, and type its **email address**.
2. opendesk shows the **provider** it found and the ways to connect, with the right one picked. Every mailbox receives by [forwarding](/guides/mailboxes/forwarding/). To send replies from your address, [Gmail and Google Workspace](/guides/mailboxes/google/) and [Microsoft 365](/guides/mailboxes/microsoft/) connect with their sign-in where it is offered, and any provider with its SMTP server, or through a [sending service](/guides/mailboxes/#send-with-a-sending-service) such as Resend or Postmark.
3. **Continue**, and follow the steps for that way in.
4. **Skip for now** opens the inbox; add a mailbox later in **Settings**, under **Mailboxes**.

</Legend>

Then turn on forwarding at your provider to the address opendesk shows. See [Connect a mailbox](/guides/mailboxes/) for each way.

</Step>
<Step title="Add live chat to your site">

Open **Settings**, then **Brands and widgets**, pick your brand and open its widget. Copy the script tag under **Install** and paste it before `</body>` on every page that should show the widget.

<Screenshot src="/guides/start/widget" alt="The chat widget settings: Brands and widgets in the settings menu ringed as 1, and the Install section with the script tag and widget key ringed as 2" />

<Legend>

1. **Brands and widgets**, under **Channels** in the settings menu.
2. **Install**: the script tag to copy, with its copy button, and the widget key.

</Legend>

To the right of these settings, a live preview shows the real widget with your unsaved changes. See [Live chat widget](/guides/live-chat/).

</Step>
<Step title="Invite your team">

Open **Settings**, then **Members**, and click **Invite people**.

<Screenshot src="/guides/start/invite" alt="The Invite people dialog: the email addresses field with two addresses ringed as 1, the roles ringed as 2, and Send invitations ringed as 3" />

<Legend>

1. **Email addresses**: type or paste several, separated by commas or spaces.
2. **Role**: **Admin** manages settings, mailboxes and members, **Agent** works tickets, contacts and articles, **Viewer** reads everything and changes nothing.
3. **Send invitations**. Each person gets an email with a link that expires after 7 days.

</Legend>

See [Teams and roles](/guides/teams-and-roles/) to group people into teams.

</Step>
<Step title="Answer your first ticket">

Send an email to your support address, or start a chat on your site. It appears in the inbox within moments. Open it, write a reply and click **Send**: the customer receives it from your address.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/open-ticket" alt="The inbox with the All open folder ringed as 1 and a ticket in the list ringed as 2" />

See [Inbox and tickets](/guides/inbox-and-tickets/) for replies, notes, status and assignment.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Keep your account safe">

Turn on [two-factor authentication](/guides/teams-and-roles/#two-factor-authentication) in **Settings**, under **Security**. As the owner, you need it for owner-level changes such as deleting the workspace.

</Callout>
