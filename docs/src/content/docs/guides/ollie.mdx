Ollie is opendesk's assistant: the violet blob in the top bar. Ask it about your queue in plain words. It reads tickets, customers, help articles, your team's [wiki](/guides/wiki/), views, reports and the team's workload, explains what it finds, and prepares changes for you. **Nothing changes until you approve it**, unless an admin lets Ollie make some changes without asking.

<Screenshot src="/guides/ollie/overview" alt="The inbox with Ollie's panel open on a ticket: the Ollie button ringed as 1, the Conversations and New chat buttons as 2, the steps Ollie took as 3, an approval card as 4 and the message box as 5" />

<Legend>

1. **Ollie**: opens and closes the panel. The shortcut is <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.
2. **Conversations** and **New chat**: your past chats with Ollie, and a fresh one.
3. **What Ollie did**: each step it took, such as reading the ticket or checking the team's workload.
4. **Approval card**: a change Ollie prepared, waiting for you.
5. **Message box**: ask anything. Type <kbd>/</kbd> for quick asks.

</Legend>

## Ask Ollie something

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Ollie">

Click **Ollie** in the top bar, or press <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> on macOS, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> elsewhere). Ollie knows which page and ticket you are looking at.

<Screenshot src="/guides/ollie/open" alt="The top bar with the Ollie button ringed as 1, next to Search and New ticket" />

</Step>
<Step title="Ask in your own words">

For example:

- "Summarize this ticket" or "Who should take this?" on an open ticket.
- "What needs my attention right now?" or "Which tickets breach SLA in the next hour?"
- "Show unassigned tickets older than a day and suggest assignees."
- "Label all billing tickets from this week."
- "Draft a reply to this customer" or "Add an internal note with what we know so far."
- "How are we doing this week?" for volume, response times, SLA and CSAT.
- "Draft a help article from recently solved tickets about the most common question."
- "What's our refund escalation process?" Ollie searches the [wiki](/guides/wiki/), reads the page and says which one the answer comes from.
- "Set my email signature to this" or "Put this signature on the support address." Ollie can change anything you can change in opendesk yourself, such as your profile, teams, addresses and settings, always with your approval. Passwords, API keys and billing stay with you, and the secrets in what Ollie reads from your settings are hidden from it, including those inside links such as a webhook's address.

Ticket numbers in Ollie's answers, like #123, open the ticket.

</Step>
<Step title="Approve or deny what it prepares">

When Ollie wants to change something, it shows an approval card with exactly what will happen: each field now and after, the tickets it covers, or the full text of a reply or note. A reply that will reach a customer is marked **Sends to the customer**. For a change in settings or in a connected product, **Show all arguments** opens the complete request, and the card says when its summary leaves something out.

<Screenshot src="/guides/ollie/approval" alt="An approval card: the assignee change from nobody to Jonas Weber ringed as 1, the Deny and Approve buttons ringed as 2" />

- **Approve** (<kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>enter</kbd>) makes the change with your permissions, once.
- **Deny** leaves everything as it is, and Ollie asks what you want instead.
- Sending a new message instead skips the change.

If an admin lets Ollie make a kind of change without asking, it makes it right away instead of showing a card, and the step is marked **Ran without asking**. **Show what it ran** opens the same fields and arguments the card would have shown.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>

Ollie works with your own role: a viewer can ask questions but not make changes, and only admins can draft automations. An approval waits 15 minutes, then lapses.

</Callout>

## Ollie on a ticket

Every ticket has an **Ollie** section in its details panel. It keeps a short summary of the conversation, offers changes, and gives shortcuts into your connected tools.

<Screenshot src="/guides/ollie/ticket-panel" alt="The Ollie section of a ticket: the summary with its key points ringed as 1, Suggest changes ringed as 2, the In your tools shortcuts ringed as 3, and the Ask Ollie about this ticket box ringed as 4" />

<Legend>

1. **Summary**: what the ticket is about, its key points and the customer's sentiment. **Refresh** writes it again.
2. **Suggest changes**: Ollie reads the ticket and proposes a priority, severity, labels or team that fit better.
3. **In your tools**: shortcuts for the products you connected, such as "Create a Linear issue". See [Integrations](/guides/integrations/).
4. **Ask Ollie about this ticket**: a question just about this conversation, answered with its sources.

</Legend>

When [triage](/guides/triage-and-assignment/) is not sure enough to apply a change on its own, it leaves it here as a suggestion. **Apply** takes it, **Skip** dismisses it.

<Screenshot src="/guides/ollie/suggest" alt="The Ollie section saying it has 1 suggestion, ringed as 1, with a severity suggestion and its Apply and Skip buttons ringed as 2" />

## Who did it

Everything the AI does in opendesk is shown as Ollie: changes you approved in the assistant, triage, drafts and AI automation actions. Chat and email answers to customers carry the name and face of the [bot](/guides/bots/) that sent them, Ollie unless you built another. A ticket's timeline reads "Ollie set severity to Sev 3" with Ollie's mark and, for triage, how sure it was. The audit log records the change as made by the AI on behalf of the person who approved it.

## Quick asks and tips

Type <kbd>/</kbd> in the message box for quick asks: summarize the open ticket, find who should take it, draft a reply, add an internal note, triage what needs attention, old unassigned tickets, how the team is doing, and draft a help article.

Now and then Ollie offers a short tip under the blob, such as "3 tickets breach SLA within the hour". Tips come from simple counts, not the AI, and cost nothing. They appear at most every 20 minutes, never while the panel is open. **Not now** hides one; **Pause for a week** stops them for a week.

</Fold>

## Conversations

Your conversations with Ollie are private to you. Open past ones from **Conversations**, rename or delete them, or start a **New chat**. The panel can sit docked beside your work or float anywhere you drag it.

</Fold>

## What Ollie can change

Ollie can change the assignee, status, priority, labels and fields, add notes, send replies you approved, save help article drafts and personal views, and (for admins) save automations, switched off for you to review. It prepares one change at a time.

Admins can let Ollie make some of these without asking, in **Settings, AI**, under **Changes Ollie makes without asking**: updating a ticket or several, adding a note, saving a draft article, a personal view or an automation (switched off). It is off by default. Ollie still makes them with the permissions of the person who asked, records each one in the audit log, and stops after 20 such changes for one message. Replies, messages to people outside the team and changes to other settings always ask first, whatever the setting says.

Bulk changes cover at most 50 tickets per approval, and the tickets are fixed when Ollie proposes the change, so a ticket that arrives later is not swept in.

With [integrations](/guides/integrations/) connected, Ollie can also look things up in Linear, GitHub, Jira, Sentry, Stripe and others, and create or update things there with the same kind of approval, marked **Runs in** the product, or without asking for the tools an admin chose.

</Fold>

## Ask a teammate's Ollie

With Collab, your own mail accounts in opendesk, much of what a teammate knows sits in their own mail. Ollie can ask a teammate's Ollie for you: "ask Priya what the Acme renewal date is". Their Ollie searches the mail they chose to share and answers on their behalf. You get the answer, never their mail, and the step shows whose Ollie answered and from how many conversations.

It is off until both sides agree:

1. An admin allows it for the workspace with **Let teammates' Ollie ask each other** in **Settings, AI**.
2. Each person opts in for their own mail in **Settings, Agent-to-agent**. Nobody's mail is reachable without their own consent, and admins cannot see or change anyone's choices.

On the agent-to-agent page you choose:

- **Share with**: everyone in the workspace, or the people and teams you pick.
- **Use information from**: all your mail accounts, or only the ones you pick. For each account choose **Verbatim** (your Ollie may quote it), **Summaries only** (the default: it answers only in its own words) or **Nothing**.
- **Sharing rules**: what your Ollie may and may not share, in your own words, such as "Share dates and next steps. Never share prices or anything personal."
- **Recent asks**: who asked, what, and what your Ollie answered, for the last 90 days.

Your Ollie also uses common sense: it leaves out personal, health, money and HR matters and anything marked confidential, declines what your rules forbid, never passes on addresses, links or attachments, and never follows instructions written in your mail. An answer that copies a summaries-only email word for word is refused.

Each answer is one call on the fast model, charged to the workspace's AI balance and counted for the person who asked.

## Cost and settings

On opendesk Cloud, each model turn of an answer is charged to the workspace's AI balance at its real cost, about $0.0012 a turn with the default model; a pricier model costs more. See [AI features](/guides/ai/#ai-usage-on-cloud).

Admins turn Ollie on or off for the workspace with **Let teammates ask Ollie** in **Settings, AI**. On a self-hosted instance Ollie needs an AI provider that supports tool use (Anthropic, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint with tools). See [AI on a self-hosted instance](/self-hosting/ai/).

</Fold>
