If you belong to more than one workspace, opendesk shows them **together** by default: every workspace's inbox, teams, views and mail in one sidebar, every notification in one bell, and the **Everywhere** lists on top. Each workspace keeps its own badge (its logo, or its initials on its own colour), so you always know where something belongs. Opening anything of another workspace takes you there at once, without reloading.

If you would rather keep work and other workspaces apart, choose **Separate**: you see one workspace at a time and switch with the workspace menu, as with a single workspace. Nothing changes for someone in one workspace.

These settings are yours alone. Billing, members, roles and settings stay per workspace.

## Choose how workspaces show

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the settings">

Go to **Settings, Account, Workspaces**, or choose **Customize sidebar** (**Arrange workspaces** when the workspace has Collab) in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step title="Pick Together or Separate">

**Together** shows every workspace at once. **Separate** shows one at a time.

</Step>
<Step title="Pick a layout">

With Together, **Grouped** gives each workspace its own foldable section, with what waits there next to its name while it is folded. **Mixed** combines them: one Inbox, one list of teams, views and mail accounts, each row led by its workspace's badge. The pages of the workspace you are in (contacts, knowledge base, reports) follow underneath.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The Everywhere lists

**Assigned to me** gathers the open tickets assigned to you in every workspace, newest activity first. **All mail** gathers the inboxes of your mail accounts in every workspace that has Collab. Each row carries its workspace's badge; opening one takes you to it in its workspace. Turn the lists off in **Settings, Account, Workspaces** if you do not use them.

## Order, hide and tidy up

- **Order**: drag a workspace by its handle, or focus the handle and press **Alt** with the up or down arrow.
- **Hide a workspace**: turn it off in the list. A hidden workspace stays out of the sidebar, the bell and the Everywhere lists, and is still one click away in the workspace menu.
- **Items**: under **Items**, choose which inbox folders, teams and views each workspace shows in the sidebar.
- **Fold**: click a workspace's name in the sidebar to fold its section; it stays folded next time.

The order of a workspace's own mail accounts and folders is set in its **Customize sidebar** dialog, as before.

## Notifications

With Together, the bell lists every workspace's notifications, each with its workspace's badge, and **Mark all read** clears them everywhere. The desktop app's badge counts them all. A notification of another workspace opens in that workspace. Links in notification emails name their workspace, so they open in the right one too.

<Callout type="note" title="Each workspace's own rules">

Every list and number is asked of each workspace separately, with your role there and its private teams. A ticket in a private team you are not on in one workspace never shows up, even next to another workspace where you can see everything.

</Callout>

## Several accounts

Together joins the workspaces of the account you are signed in with. If you are signed in to more than one account in the same browser, each account's workspaces stay with that account, and you switch accounts with the workspace menu.
