Every conversation in opendesk is a **ticket**. Email, live chat, the API and the help center portal all create tickets in the same inbox, so your team works one queue. The inbox updates live: when a teammate replies, changes a status or assigns a ticket, everyone sees it without refreshing.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/overview" alt="The inbox: folders and views in the sidebar, the ticket list, and an open email ticket with its timeline, reply box and details panel, each part numbered" />

<Legend>

1. **Folders**: Unassigned, Mine, All open, Pending, Solved and Spam, each with a live count.
2. **Views**: saved filters your team shares, like Urgent or Billing queue. See [Saved replies and views](/guides/saved-replies-and-views/).
3. **Ticket list**: the tickets in the folder or view, with search, filter and sort at the top.
4. **Ticket actions**: **Solve**, **Snooze**, and the **⋯** menu to assign to yourself, merge, copy a link, mark as spam or delete.
5. **Timeline**: the customer's messages, your replies, internal notes and every change, in order.
6. **Reply box**: write to the customer, or switch to **Note** to write only for your team.
7. **Details**: status, priority, severity, assignee, team, brand, the address replies go out from, and labels.

</Legend>

## Answer a ticket

<Steps>
<Step title="Open a folder, then a ticket">

Pick a folder or a view in the sidebar, then click a ticket in the list. Use the arrows at the top of the ticket, or <kbd>j</kbd> and <kbd>k</kbd>, to move to the next one.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/open-ticket" alt="The inbox with the All open folder ringed as 1 and a ticket in the list ringed as 2" />

</Step>
<Step title="Write a reply or a note">

**Reply** writes to the customer. On email tickets it is sent from the ticket's mailbox with a signature (the team's, else the address's, else your own), so the customer sees your support address. **Note** writes an internal note that customers never see, over email or in the chat widget. Type <kbd>/</kbd> to insert a saved reply, or `{{` to insert a [variable](/guides/email-templates/variables/) such as the customer's first name.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/reply" alt="The reply box with a drafted reply: the Reply and Note tabs ringed as 1, the Send button and its menu ringed as 2" />

</Step>
<Step title="Send, and set the status in one go">

Click **Send**, or press <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>enter</kbd>. The arrow next to it sends and sets the status in the same step: **Send and mark pending**, **Send and mark on hold**, or **Send and mark solved** (<kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>shift</kbd> <kbd>enter</kbd>). Each reply then shows its delivery status: pending, sent, failed or bounced.

</Step>
<Step title="Check who else is on the ticket">

On an open ticket you see who else is viewing or replying, and on chat tickets the customer's typing. If a teammate is already replying, the bar above the reply box says so, and **Leave a note instead** switches you to a note.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/presence" alt="The bar above the reply box ringed: Priya is replying to this ticket right now, with Leave a note instead" />

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>

- <kbd>mod</kbd> is <kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> elsewhere. Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere for every shortcut, or see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/keyboard-shortcuts/).
- Attachments upload once and are linked to the message when it is sent.
- A new reply from the customer reopens a solved ticket.

</Callout>

## Set status, priority and assignment

Everything about a ticket is in the details panel on the right. Click a value to change it; the change shows up for the whole team at once and is recorded in the timeline.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/details" alt="The details panel: status, priority and severity ringed as 1, assignee and team ringed as 2, labels ringed as 3" />

<Legend>

1. **Status**, **priority** (low, normal, high or urgent) and an optional **severity**, from Sev 1 (critical) to Sev 4 (low impact), shown as a compact chip such as `S2`.
2. **Assignee** and **team**. Assign a person, a team, or both. Assigning someone notifies them (see [Notifications](/guides/notifications/)).
3. **Labels**, to group and find tickets. See [Labels and custom fields](/guides/labels-and-custom-fields/).

</Legend>

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `open` | Waiting on your team. |
| `pending` | Waiting on the customer. |
| `on_hold` | Waiting on something else, such as another team or a third party. |
| `solved` | Resolved. A new reply from the customer reopens the conversation. |
| `closed` | Finished and kept for the record. |

A workspace can also assign new tickets automatically, by round robin or by load, in **Settings**, under **Assignment**, and [automations](/guides/automations/) can assign by rule. See [Triage and assignment](/guides/triage-and-assignment/).

## Snooze, merge, spam and delete

**Snooze** hides a ticket until a time you pick: in an hour, in four hours, tomorrow, next week, or a date and time of your own. A snoozed ticket is on hold until then.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/snooze" alt="The Snooze button ringed as 1 and its menu ringed as 2: In 1 hour, In 4 hours, Tomorrow, Next week, or a date" />

The **⋯** menu holds the rest: **Merge into another ticket**, **Copy link**, **Mark as spam** and **Delete ticket**.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/more-actions" alt="The More actions button ringed as 1 and its menu ringed as 2: Merge into another ticket, Copy link, Mark as spam and Delete ticket" />

## Work on many tickets at once

Hover a ticket and tick its box (or press <kbd>x</kbd>), then tick any others. The bar at the bottom of the list changes them all in one step: assignee, team, status, priority, severity, labels, spam, or delete.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/bulk" alt="The ticket list with two tickets ticked, ringed as 1, and the bulk actions bar at the bottom ringed as 2" />

## Find tickets

The **filter** button at the top of the list narrows it by priority, severity, channel, label, team, brand, address or custom field. Save a filter you use often as a [view](/guides/saved-replies-and-views/), and it appears in the sidebar with its own count.

<Screenshot src="/guides/inbox/filter" alt="The filter button ringed at the top of the ticket list, with its menu open: Priority, Severity, Channel, Label, Team, Brand, Address and Fields" />

Press <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>k</kbd>, or click **Search** at the top, to search everything at once: tickets, contacts, articles and settings.

## What a ticket holds

- a **number** (`#1042`), unique and increasing within the workspace
- a **subject** and a **channel**: `email`, `chat`, `api` or `portal`
- a **status**, a **priority** and an optional **severity**
- an optional **assignee**, **team**, **mailbox** and **labels**
- the **contact** who wrote in, and their company when known
- an **SLA** with first response and resolution due times, when a policy applies (see [SLA policies](/guides/sla-policies/))
- a **CSAT** score once the customer rates the conversation (see [CSAT](/guides/csat/))

</Fold>

## Replies, notes and activity

A ticket's timeline holds its messages and its activity:

- **Replies** go to the customer. Each one shows its delivery status: pending, sent, failed or bounced.
- **Notes** are internal. Customers never see them, over email or in the chat widget.
- **Side conversations** are email conversations with someone outside your team, such as a supplier or a payment provider. See [Ask someone outside your team](#ask-someone-outside-your-team).
- **Activity** records status, priority, severity, assignment, team, label, SLA, merge and CSAT changes, with who made them.

### Ask someone outside your team

Some tickets need an answer from someone else first: a supplier, a logistics partner, a payment provider. Start a **side conversation** from the ticket instead of writing from your own inbox:

1. In the reply box, switch to **Side conversation** (or run **Start a side conversation** from the command menu, or click **New** under **Side conversations** in the ticket details).
2. Enter who to write to and your message. The subject starts as the ticket's; change it if it should read differently for them. Saved replies work here too, without the customer's or the ticket's details; Ollie's drafting is off, since it writes from the conversation with the customer.
3. Only your message goes out unless you add more: open the menu next to **Send** and, under **Include**, tick **The conversation** to quote the last messages with the customer (up to 10) below yours, or any of the files they sent. What you add shows next to your attachments, where you can take it out again. Internal notes never go along.
4. Send. To wait for them, choose **Send and mark on hold** from the send menu.

It is emailed from the ticket's support address, with your signature and only what you chose to include. Their answers come back into the same ticket, in the timeline and under **Side conversations** in the ticket details, and the customer never sees any of it. When they answer:

- The ticket's assignee is notified.
- A ticket waiting on the customer or on hold opens again, so you can pick it up.
- Nothing counts as a customer reply: SLA targets, response times, reports and automations are not affected, and no auto-reply goes out.

**Reply** from the latest message of a side conversation to write in it again. **Close** it when you have what you need; a new message opens it again.

</Fold>

## Contacts and companies

Every customer is a **contact**, matched by email address, or by your own user id when the chat widget is [verified](/guides/live-chat/identity-verification/). Contacts can belong to a **company**, carry notes and custom fields, and be blocked. A contact's page lists all of their tickets. Open **Contacts** in the sidebar to browse them.
